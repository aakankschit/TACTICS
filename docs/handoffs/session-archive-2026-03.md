- **2026-03-16 (session 3)**: Ran thrombin warmup comparison (7 methods × 20 replicates, 29,220 budget). **Hypothesis rejected**: Balanced warmup significantly hurts — Balanced-TT-TS=72.5 vs Enhanced-TT-TS=78.0 (-5.5, p<0.0001), Balanced-RWS=73.5 vs Enhanced-RWS=78.5 (-5.0, p<0.0001). Manuscript methods confirmed as optimal. New open question: direct uncertainty-driven diversity sampling as alternative to thermal cycling (future work, not this manuscript).
